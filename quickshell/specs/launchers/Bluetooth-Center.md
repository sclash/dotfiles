# Bluetooth-Center

- ***Command: *** `SUPER + b`

It should pop up window structured as such:

- What devices the machine is currently connected to showing:
    - Device Name
    - Signal intensity
    - I should be able to diconnect from the current network.

- I should see the following options:
    - Known devices
    - Scan for devices

- ***Known Devices: *** 
    - All know devices
    - Wether or not are available
    - Forget a device
    - Every new connected device should be automatically added to the list of known
    devices

- ***Scan Networks: *** 
    - All devices available with names
    - Connect to a device being automatically prompted to enter a password
